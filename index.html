A year-long campaign · 2026–2027

Reading well is how a person keeps learning for life.

#LiftingLiteracy connects literacy organizations with the communities they serve and widens access to reading for children, teens and adults.

Members

Free to join, for readers of every age and the people who help them.

Members are individuals: parents, grandparents, teens, adult learners, tutors, teachers and anyone who cares about reading.

Become a member

What members get

  • Newsletter updates on the campaign, new resources and upcoming events.

  • The online Resource Library of guides, book lists and recordings.

  • Discount prices on live events such as conferences and webinars.

Partners

Find a partner and give directly.

Partners are literacy organizations: schools, libraries, after-school programs, tutoring programs, home schools, families and community groups. Each one runs its own fundraiser. Every gift goes straight to the partner you choose. #LiftingLiteracy does not collect or hold donations.
