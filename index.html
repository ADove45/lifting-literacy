Comprehension is the second reading

When Mae opened the letter, she read it twice. The first time she read the words. The second time she read what they meant.

Why twice?What changed between readings?

Why literacy

Sounding out the words is the start. Understanding them is the skill.

Reading comprehension carries into every class, every job application, every lease, every ballot and every prescription label. It is the basis for lifelong learning and for everyday life skills. This campaign is for readers of every age.

Children

The joy of reading comes first: stories that make children want to turn one page, then another.

Teens

Learning to read and comprehend is critical now, because from here on teens read to learn in every subject.

Adults

Reading with confidence at work, at home and in the community. It is never too late.

How the campaign works

Many organizations, one shared year of reading.

  1. Literacy organizations join as partners. Schools, libraries, after-school programs, tutoring programs, home schools, families, nonprofits and community groups that help people read.

  2. Each partner runs its own fundraiser and adds a #LiftingLiteracy page to its own website.

  3. This site connects them. One place to find every partner, give to the one you choose, and join as a member.

Where your gift goes

Every gift goes straight to the partner you choose. #LiftingLiteracy does not collect or hold donations.
