Tell us what you need

Your reading goals, in your words.

We want to hear from the readers this campaign is for. Tell us what you want to do and what would help. It takes about 3 minutes.

Good to know

  • Your name is not required. You can share your name, location or contact information at the end if you would like us to follow up.

  • Someone can help you. A friend, family member or tutor can read the questions aloud and fill this in with you.

  • There are no wrong answers. Skip any question you like.
