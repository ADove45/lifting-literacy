Become a #LiftingLiteracy partner.

Join a year-long campaign with other organizations that help people read, and keep every dollar you raise.

Apply as a partner
Who can join
Schools, libraries, after-school programs, tutoring programs, home schools, families, nonprofits and community groups whose work helps children, teens or adults read with understanding.
Where donations go
Straight to you. #LiftingLiteracy does not collect or hold donations.
Cost to join
To confirm

What you get

A place in the campaign.

  • A page on this site with your story, your logo and a button to your fundraiser.

  • A ready-made #LiftingLiteracy page to add to your own website.

  • Campaign logo and badge files for your site, emails and social posts.

  • A listing in the partner directory, where visitors choose who to give to.

What we ask

Three commitments.

  • Add a #LiftingLiteracy page to your website that links back to this site.

  • Run your own fundraiser and keep its link up to date.

  • Use the campaign name and logo as provided.

To confirm whether partners also report their results for a campaign total.

How to join

From application to live page.

  1. Apply with the partner form.

  2. Send your details: logo, a short description of your literacy work, and your fundraiser link.

  3. Your page goes up on this site and in the partner directory.

  4. Add the kit page to your website and send us its address.

  5. The two pages link to each other, and you are part of the campaign.

The partner kit

Everything for your own website

  • A page template with the campaign wording and a space for your story.

  • A plain copy-and-paste version for sites that cannot take custom code.

  • Logo and badge files.

  • Short instructions for WordPress, Squarespace and Wix.
