Borrow the books

Read them free with Libby.

Libby is the free app public libraries use to lend ebooks and audiobooks. All you need is a library card. Every book list in the library will link to its titles in Libby.

Get Libby

How to borrow

  1. Get a library card from your public library. Many libraries let you sign up online.

  2. Install Libby and add your card.

  3. Choose a title from a book list here and select Borrow with Libby.

Free to use today

Free reading resources you can use right now.

While our own shelves fill up, here are trusted, free places to read, listen and learn. These links go to other organizations' websites.

For everyone

Books and audiobooks, free with a library card or no card at all.

Hoopla

Ebooks, audiobooks, comics and movies you borrow instantly with your public library card. Ask your library if it offers Hoopla.

Project Gutenberg

Tens of thousands of classic books in the public domain, free to read online or download. No account needed.

Visit Project Gutenberg (opens in a new tab) ↗

LibriVox

Free audiobooks of public domain classics, read aloud by volunteers. Listen online or download.

Visit LibriVox (opens in a new tab) ↗

For children

The joy of reading, at home and in class.

Storyline Online

Picture books read aloud on video, with the pictures on screen, from the SAG-AFTRA Foundation.

Visit Storyline Online (opens in a new tab) ↗

Unite for Literacy

Free picture books online, narrated in more than 50 languages. No logins and no ads.

Visit Unite for Literacy (opens in a new tab) ↗

Dolly Parton's Imagination Library

A free book mailed to your child every month from birth to age 5, whatever your income. South Carolina families can check eligibility with First Five SC.

For teens and adults

Reading to learn, and reading with confidence.

CommonLit

Free reading passages for grades 3 to 12, each with questions that check understanding.

Visit CommonLit (opens in a new tab) ↗

ReadTheory

Free reading comprehension practice that adjusts to your level, with feedback on every answer. Adult-friendly. Free account required.

Visit ReadTheory (opens in a new tab) ↗

USA Learns

Free online courses for adults learning English, including reading stories with comprehension questions. Free sign-up required.
