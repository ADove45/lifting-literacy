Before you start

Most of these are free with no account. A few use your public library card, which is also free. These links go to other organizations' websites.

Borrow the books

Read them free with Libby.

Libby is the free app public libraries use to lend ebooks and audiobooks. All you need is a library card. Many of the books on our future reading lists will be available there too.

Get Libby

How to borrow

  1. Get a library card from your public library. Many libraries let you sign up online.

  2. Install Libby and add your card.

  3. Search for a title and select Borrow. It is yours free for the loan period.
